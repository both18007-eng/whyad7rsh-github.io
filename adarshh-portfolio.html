K20 Pro Flash Guide

Redmi K20 Pro (raphael). Tap Copy on any command.

Unlocking wipes all data and can affect warranty. Back up everything first.

1. Unlock bootloader

Add a Mi account in Developer options, turn on OEM unlocking and USB debugging, apply for permission, then unlock with Mi Unlock Tool (hold Volume Down + Power for fastboot).

adb reboot bootloader
fastboot devices
fastboot flashing unlock
2. Flash a custom ROM

Boot a recovery (TWRP or OrangeFox), push the ROM zip, wipe if you are switching ROM families, flash the ROM and then GApps if needed, and reboot. First boot can take several minutes.

fastboot boot recovery.img
adb push rom.zip /sdcard/
adb sideload rom.zip
fastboot reboot
3. Relock bootloader

Flash the latest official stock fastboot ROM first, then relock.

adb reboot bootloader
fastboot oem lock
Never relock with a custom ROM or recovery installed. It is the most common cause of a hard brick.

4. Back to stock ROM

Download the official fastboot ROM for raphael, extract it, open Mi Flash Tool, choose the folder, then pick “clean all” or “save user data” and flash.

adb reboot bootloader
fastboot getvar all
fastboot reboot
